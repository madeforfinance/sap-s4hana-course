Part 1

Tolerance — The Basic Idea

Video 08:06:10 · Tolerance

Tolerance kya hai? (Simple words)

Tolerance ka matlab hai: "kitni had tak ki chhoti ghalti / chhota farq system qubool kar lega". English mein tolerance ka matlab hi "bardasht" (برداشت) hota hai — kitna farq bardasht hai.

Real life mein dekhein: aap ne vendor ko Rs 100,000 dene thay, bank ne Rs 99,995 bhej diye (Rs 5 kam). Ya invoice Rs 50,000 ka hai lekin goods Rs 49,980 ke receive hue. Ab sawal: kya har chhote farq par system ruk jaye aur cheekhe "ERROR! Amount match nahi ho raha"? Agar aisa ho to accountant ka aadha din sirf Rs 2–5 ke farq peeche zaya hoga.

Isi liye SAP mein pehle se limit set kar di jati hai: "Agar farq is limit ke andar hai, to system khud adjust kar lega — chhota sa difference ek alag account mein daal dega aur kaam aage barh jayega. Agar farq limit se bara hai, to system rok dega."

Analogy — Sabzi wala aur khullay paisay Aap sabzi lene gaye, bill bana Rs 498, aap ne Rs 500 diye. Sabzi wala kehta hai "Bhai, Rs 2 rehne do, khullay nahi hain." Ye Rs 2 aap ki tolerance hai — itna chhota farq aap ne bardasht kar liya. Lekin bill Rs 400 ka ho aur wo Rs 500 rakh le, to aap bardasht nahi karenge — ye tolerance se bahar hai. SAP bilkul yehi karta hai.

Tolerance SAP mein kahan kahan use hoti hai?

#Tolerance ki qisamKahan kaam aati hai
1Employee Tolerance GroupHar user/accountant kitni bari amount post kar sakta hai, aur payment mein kitna farq maaf kar sakta hai
2G/L Account Tolerance GroupG/L accounts ki clearing (jaise bank clearing, suspense clearing) mein kitna farq chalega
3Vendor / Customer Tolerance GroupVendor ya customer ke payments mein farq, discount ki limit, residual items ki rules
4Payment DifferencePayment kam ya zyada aye to farq ko handle karna (Section 3)
Memory Trick Tolerance = "Bardasht ki limit". Teen groups yaad rakhein — Banday (Employee), Khata (G/L), Party (Vendor/Customer). Exam mein jab "tolerance" ka sawal aye, pehle ye sochiye: farq kis ke hisaab mein hai — user ke, G/L ke, ya vendor/customer ke?

Ek chhota sa number example

Vendor invoice (bill)      = Rs 100,000
Payment jo bheji           = Rs  99,900
Farq (difference)          = Rs      100

Tolerance limit set hai    = Rs      500 tak

Farq (100) < Limit (500)   → System: "OK, chalega."
                             Rs 100 ko "Payment Difference"
                             account mein daal kar invoice CLEAR.

Agar payment Rs 98,000 hoti → Farq Rs 2,000 > Limit 500
                             → System ROK dega. Pehle farq ki
                               wajah (reason) dena hogi.

Part 2

Tolerance Groups: Employee, G/L, Vendor & Customer

Video 08:06:10 – 08:59:26 · Configuration (SPRO)

SPRO yaad hai? (Lesson 3/5) — ye SAP ka configuration area hai, T-code SPRO. Tolerance groups bhi wahin bante hain. Har group ek 4-character code hota hai jo company code ke saath link hota hai.

2.1 — Employee Tolerance Group OBA4

Simple matlab: Ye har user (accountant) ki "power ki limit" hai. Is group mein ye cheezein set hoti hain:

  • Amount per document — ek document mein total kitni bari rakam post kar sakta hai (misal: 1,000,000 tak).
  • Amount per open item — ek line item kitna bara ho sakta hai.
  • Payment difference (amount + %) — payment clear karte waqt kitna farq (rupees mein bhi, percent mein bhi) khud adjust kar sakta hai.
  • Cash discount % — payment par kitne percent tak discount khud de sakta hai.

Kaise assign hota hai? Tolerance group har user ko us ke user master record mein assign hota hai (user administration, T-code SU01). Agar user ko koi group assign na ho, to blank (empty) group lagta hai — aur blank group sab se pehle define karna zaroori hai, warna posting par error aata hai "No tolerance group found".

Exam Trap #1 Agar company code ke liye blank tolerance group define na ho to har posting fail hogi, kyunke jin users ko koi group assign nahi, system unhein blank group mein dhoondta hai. Exam mein aksar sawal aata hai: "User ko error aa raha hai halanke amount chhoti hai — kyun?" Jawab: blank employee tolerance group missing hai.

2.2 — G/L Account Tolerance Group OBA0

Simple matlab: Ye G/L accounts ke liye hai — khaas taur par un accounts ke liye jin ki clearing hoti hai, jaise Bank Clearing Account, GR/IR Clearing Account, Suspense Account. Jab in accounts ke debit/credit mein chhota farq reh jaye, to tolerance group decide karta hai ke farq automatically ek difference account mein post ho jaye ya nahi.

Ye group G/L master record (FS00) ke Control Data tab mein lagta hai — field ka naam: Tolerance Group.

2.3 — Vendor & Customer Tolerance Group OBA3

Simple matlab: Ye har vendor ya customer party ke liye rules hain: payment mein kitna farq chalega, kitna discount chalega, aur farq ko handle kaise hoga — khud adjust (write off) ya naya baaqi (residual item) bana kar.

Ye group vendor master (XK02 / Business Partner) ke Payment Transactions view mein aur customer master mein assign hota hai.

OBA3 mein khaas fields:

  • Permitted payment differences — amount aur % dono. Dono check hote hain: farq amount limit aur % limit — dono ke andar hona chahiye.
  • Tolerances for payment advices — customer jo payment advice (detail) bhejta hai, us se farq ki limit.
  • Residual items from payment differences — agar ye tick ho, to tolerance se bahar / khaas farq ke liye naya residual item banega (Section 3).
  • Discount adjustment — discount ki chhoti ghalti ko adjust karna hai ya nahi.
  • Tolerance for terms of payment — payment terms ki date mein kitne din ki dheel (days) chalegi.

Teeno groups ek nazar mein

GroupT-codeTableKis par lagta haiKya control karta hai
Employee ToleranceOBA4T043TUser (user master parameters)Max document amount, max open-item amount, payment difference, discount %
G/L ToleranceOBA0T043SG/L account master (FS00)G/L clearing mein difference ki limit

Memory Trick — T-codes OBA0 = G/L (0 sab se pehla, G/L sab se basic), OBA3 = Vendor/Customer (3 = third party!), OBA4 = Employee (user). Yaad karein: "0 = Khata, 3 = Party, 4 = Banda".
Note — Kaun si tolerance pehle check hoti hai? Payment clear karte waqt SAP sab se sakht (sab se chhoti) limit apply karta hai — employee tolerance aur vendor/customer tolerance dono check hoti hain. Agar ek mein bhi farq limit se bahar ho, document clear nahi hoga. Ye bhi ek mashhoor exam point hai.

Part 3

Payment Differences, Residual & Partial Payments

Video 08:06:10 – 08:59:26 · Tolerance ka practical use

Payment Difference kya hota hai?

Jab payment ki amount aur invoice ki amount barabar na ho, farq ko payment difference kehte hain. Farq do tarah ka ho sakta hai: payment kam (underpayment) ya payment zyada (overpayment). Farq ki wajahen: bank charges kat gaye, rounding, discount liya gaya, ya goods mein kuch kami thi.

Farq ko handle karne ke 3 tareeqay

TareeqaSimple matlabInvoice ka kya hota hai
1. Write off (adjust)Chhota farq tolerance ke andar hai — system farq ko "Gain/Loss on payment difference" account mein daal deta haiInvoice poora clear ho jata hai
2. Residual ItemJo amount baaqi reh gayi, us ki ek nayi chhoti open entry ban jati hai; purani invoice bandInvoice clear, lekin naya open item baaqi amount ka ban jata hai
3. Partial PaymentJitni payment ayi, wo alag open item ban jati hai; invoice khuli (open) rehti haiInvoice bhi open, payment bhi open — dono list mein nazar aate hain
Analogy — Udhaar ki diary Dost ko Rs 10,000 udhaar diye thay. Residual: wo Rs 7,000 wapas karta hai — aap purani entry kaat kar nayi entry likhte hain "Rs 3,000 baaqi". Diary mein ab sirf ek saaf entry hai. Partial: aap purani Rs 10,000 wali entry rehne dete hain aur neeche ek aur entry likhte hain "Rs 7,000 wasool". Diary mein ab do entries hain jin ka net Rs 3,000 hai. Write off: wo Rs 9,980 deta hai aur aap kehte hain "Rs 20 chhoro yaar" — hisaab band.

Reason Codes — farq ki wajah batana

Jab farq tolerance se bara ho, ya company chahti ho ke har farq ki wajah record ho, to Reason Code use hota hai. Reason code ek chhota sa code hai jo batata hai farq kyun aya — misal: bank charges, damaged goods, price difference. Reason code ke saath ek G/L account link hota hai taake farq auto usi account mein jaye. Configuration: OBBE (reason codes define karna).

Cash Discount aur Tolerance

Cash discount = jaldi payment karne par milne wali chhoot (misal: 2% agar 10 din mein payment ho). Agar customer discount ki date guzarne ke baad bhi discount kaat le, aur farq tolerance ke andar ho, to SAP usay "unauthorized discount" ke tor par adjust kar sakta hai — ye setting bhi OBA3 / OBA4 mein hoti hai (Adjust discount fields).

MISAL — teeno tareeqay ek saath:
Invoice amount            = Rs 50,000
Payment ayi               = Rs 47,000
Farq                      = Rs  3,000   (tolerance Rs 500 se BARA)

Option A — Residual Item:
  Invoice Rs 50,000  → CLEARED
  Naya open item     → Rs 3,000 (baaqi, baad mein wasool hoga)

Option B — Partial Payment:
  Invoice Rs 50,000  → OPEN (ab bhi list mein)
  Payment Rs 47,000  → OPEN (alag item)
  Dono ka net        → Rs 3,000 baaqi

Option C — Write off: sirf tab jab farq tolerance
  ke ANDAR ho (misal farq Rs 300 hota to farq
  difference account mein, invoice clear).
Exam Trap #2 Residual item mein original invoice clear ho jati hai aur naya item banta hai — is naye item ki payment terms aur due date naye hisaab se chalti hain. Partial payment mein original invoice open rehti hai aur us ki original due date hi rehti hai. Ye farq exam mein baar baar poocha jata hai.

Post & Park Document

Video 08:59:26 · Post & Park Document & G/L Reporting

Park karna kya hota hai?

Accounting mein har entry seedha final nahi hoti. Aksar ek junior accountant entry banata hai aur senior/manager pehle check karta hai, phir final hoti hai. SAP mein is check-wali darmiyani halat ko Parking kehte hain.

Parked document = ek aisi entry jo system mein save hai, us ka document number bhi mil gaya hai, lekin wo abhi accounts par koi asar nahi daalti — na balance change hota hai, na reports mein aati hai. Jab senior approve kare, to usi document ko Post kar diya jata hai — tab wo asal entry ban jati hai.

Analogy — WhatsApp message ke 3 darjay Hold = message likha, phone ki screen par hai, bheja hi nahi (draft bhi save nahi). Park = message "draft" mein save kar diya — maujood hai, lekin gaya nahi. Post = Send dab gaya — ab message pohonch gaya, wapas nahi ho sakta (sirf reverse/delete-for-everyone jaisa option hai, asal mein entry reversal se hoti hai).

Post vs Park vs Hold — full comparison

Key Term — Four-Eyes Principle Four-eyes principle (chaar aankhein) matlab: jo shakhs entry banaye, wo khud usay final na kare — dusri "do aankhein" (senior) check karein. Ye fraud aur ghalti rokne ka mashhoor control hai. Parking isi principle ko SAP mein mumkin banati hai. Exam mein "park document ka faida" poocha jaye to yehi jawab hai.

Workflow — automatic approval

Bari companies mein park kiye hue documents ke liye Workflow lagta hai — ek automatic system jo parked document ko khud senior ki inbox mein bhej deta hai; senior "Release" kare to document post hone ke qabil ho jata hai. Is ke liye release amount limit set hoti hai: misal Rs 100,000 se upar ki parked entry ko pehle ek level ki release, us se upar doosre level ki. Configuration OBWA / OBWJ / OBWK (workflow variants) mein hoti hai — C_TS4FI mein is ka basic concept poocha jata hai, gehri setting nahi.

Exam Trap #3 (1) Parked document ka document number asal number range se milta hai — hold document ka nahi. (2) Parked document balance update nahi karta, lekin wo reports ki kuch jagah (parked documents list) mein nazar aata hai. (3) Parked document ko delete kar sakte hain, posted ko nahi — posted sirf reverse (FB08) hota hai.

F-02 — General Posting, Step by Step

Video 10:01:57 · F-02 Document Posting

F-02 kya hai?

F-02 SAP ki sab se purani aur classic journal entry screen hai — naam hai "Enter G/L Account Document / General Posting". FB50 (Lesson 3/9) is ka asaan "enjoy" version hai, lekin F-02 aaj bhi use hoti hai kyunke ye har qisam ki posting kar sakti hai: G/L, vendor, customer — sab ek hi screen se, posting keys ke zariye.

Analogy — Manual register vs form FB50 ek ready-made form hai — khane bane hue hain, bas bhar dein. F-02 purana khula register hai — aap ko khud har line likhni parti hai aur har line ke saath code (posting key) batana parta hai ke ye debit hai ya credit, G/L hai ya vendor. Mushkil zyada, lekin control bhi zyada.

Posting Key kya hoti hai? (Pehli baar aaya hai to ghaur se)

Posting Key ek 2-digit code hai jo har line item ko batata hai: (1) ye Debit hai ya Credit, aur (2) ye kis qisam ka account hai — G/L, Vendor ya Customer. Configuration: OB41.

Posting KeyDebit / CreditAccount typeUse

Memory Trick — Posting Keys 40/50 G/L ki jori hai (40 Debit, 50 Credit) — ye do sab se zyada use hote hain. Vendor hamesha 1 par khatam nahi... asaan trick: "Vendor Credit = 31 (teen-ik: party ko dena)", "Customer Debit = 01 (shuruaat customer se)". Aur note karein: odd-ish pehle digits (0,2) debit side, (1,3,5) credit side ki taraf ishara karte hain — 40 is rule ka exception hai, isay alag yaad karein: 40 Debit, 50 Credit ratta laga lein.

Step by step — Rent ki entry F-02 mein

  1. Command field mein F-02 likh kar Enter. Header screen khulegi.
  2. Document Date (asal kagaz ki date), Posting Date (kis period mein entry lagegi — Lesson 6 ka posting period yaad karein), Document Type SA, Company Code, Currency bharein.
  3. Pehli line: Posting Key 40 + G/L account 630000 (Rent Expense) → Enter. Line-item screen mein amount 75,000, cost center, aur text "Office rent Nov 2026" likhein.
  4. Agli line: Posting Key 50 + Bank/Cash G/L account → amount 75,000 credit.
  5. Simulate karein (Document → Simulate): system poori entry dikhata hai — Debit 75,000 = Credit 75,000, farq 0.
  6. Post (Save / Ctrl+S) — document number mil jata hai, misal "Document 100000123 was posted in company code 1000".

Exam Trap #5 (1) F-02 mein debit = credit hona lazmi hai, warna document post nahi hoga — system farq dikhata rahega. (2) Document Date ≠ Posting Date: document date kagaz ki date hai, posting date decide karti hai entry kis period/fiscal year mein jayegi. (3) F-02 se vendor/customer line bhi lag sakti hai (PK 31/01) — ye sirf G/L screen nahi hai, naam "General Posting" is liye hai.

Part 7

Accounts Payable — Vendor Master & Setup

Video 10:06:40 · Accounts Payable shuru

Accounts Payable (AP) kya hai?

Accounts Payable matlab: "wo paisay jo company ne dusron ko DENE hain" — khaas taur par apne vendors (suppliers) ko, jin se hum ne samaan ya service udhaar par li hai. Aap ki Financial Accounting mein yehi cheez Trade Payables / Creditors kehlati hai. SAP mein AP, FI module ka ek sub-ledger hai — har vendor ka alag tafseeli khata (ledger), lekin total hamesha G/L se milta hai.

Analogy — Kiryana shop ki udhaar copy Mohallay ki dukaan par har ghar ki ek alag udhaar copy hoti hai — kis ne kya liya, kitna baaqi hai. Lekin dukaan ke main register mein sirf ek total likha hota hai "kul udhaar baaqi: Rs 85,000". SAP mein vendor ki copy = AP sub-ledger, main register = G/L reconciliation account. Dono ka total hamesha barabar — yehi SAP ki khoobi hai, copy mein entry lagi to register khud update.

Vendor Master — vendor ki file

Kisi vendor ke saath kaam shuru karne se pehle SAP mein us ki master file banti hai. S/4HANA mein vendor Business Partner (BP) ke roop mein banta hai (T-code BP), purane T-codes XK01 (create), XK02 (change), XK03 (display) bhi chalte hain. Vendor master ke 3 hisse hote hain:

HissaKis level parKya hota hai
General DataClient level (sab ke liye ek)Naam, address, phone, tax numbers (NTN), bank ki bunyadi maloomat
Company Code DataHar company code ke liye alagReconciliation account, payment terms, payment methods, tolerance group, house bank
Purchasing DataPurchasing organization levelOrder currency, delivery terms — ye MM (Materials Management) ka hissa hai

Reconciliation Account — AP ka dil

Reconciliation account (short: recon account) wo G/L account hai (misal 211000 — Trade Payables) jo vendor master mein lagta hai. Is ke rules:

  • Vendor par jo bhi entry lagegi (invoice, payment), us ka total khud-ba-khud is G/L account mein update hoga.
  • Is account mein seedhi (direct) posting nahi ho sakti — F-02/FB50 se is mein entry lagane jao to system rokega: "Account is a reconciliation account". Entry hamesha vendor ke through hogi.
  • Isi liye Balance Sheet mein "Trade Payables" ki figure hamesha saare vendors ke baaqi ke total ke barabar hoti hai — kabhi farq nahi aa sakta.
  • Recon account G/L master (FS00) mein field Account Type... asal mein G/L ke "Type/Description" mein recon account ki qisam K (Vendors) set hoti hai.

Vendor Account Group — vendor ki qisam

Vendors ko groups mein baanta jata hai — Account Group (OBD3), misal: KRED = local vendors, ZDOM = domestic, one-time vendor ke liye CPD. Account group decide karta hai: number range (vendor ka number kahan se kahan tak), aur master mein kaun se fields zaroori/chhupe hue (field status — Lesson 7 wali hi soch).

Key Term — One-Time Vendor (CPD) Jis vendor se sirf ek baar kaam ho (misal: ek dafa plumber), us ke liye poora master banana fazool hai. One-time vendor ek dummy master hota hai; naam/address invoice lagate waqt entry screen par hi likh diya jata hai.

Payment Terms — kab dene hain paisay?

Payment Terms (OBB8) wo shartein hain jo batati hain payment kab due hogi aur jaldi dene par kitna discount milega. Misal mashhoor term Z030: "30 din mein payment, 14 din mein do to 2% discount".

  • Baseline Date — wo date jisse din ginna shuru hote hain (aam tor par document date ya posting date; terms mein default set hota hai).
  • Cash discount % + days — pehli line: X din mein payment → Y% discount; doosri line: net due date (bina discount aakhri tareekh).
  • Payment terms vendor master mein default lagti hain; invoice par change bhi ho sakti hain.
  • Due date = Baseline date + terms ke din. AP reports mein "due / overdue" isi se nikalta hai.
PAYMENT TERM "Z030" KI MISAL:
Baseline date (invoice date) : 01 Nov 2026
2% discount agar payment      : 14 Nov 2026 tak  (14 din)
Net due date (aakhri tareekh) : 01 Dec 2026      (30 din)

Rs 100,000 ke bill par:
  14 Nov tak do  → Rs 98,000 do (2,000 discount)
  Us ke baad do  → poore Rs 100,000
Exam Trap #6 (1) Vendor master ka company code data har company code ke liye alag banta hai — recon account aur payment terms company-code-wise mukhtalif ho sakte hain. (2) Recon account mein direct posting impossible hai. (3) S/4HANA mein vendor = Business Partner with role FI Vendor (FLVN00) — naya vendor BP se banta hai, XK01 purana tareeqa hai jo BP par hi le jata hai.

Step 3 — Vendor invoice post karna

Do mashhoor tareeqay:

  • FB60 — Enter Incoming Invoice: seedha FI mein vendor ka bill lagana (bina purchase order ke, misal: bijli ka bill, rent). Entry: Expense Dr / Vendor Cr.
  • MIRO — Logistics Invoice Verification: jab purchase order aur goods receipt pehle se hon (3-way match: PO = GR = Invoice). Ye MM-FI ka mila hua area hai.
FB60 ENTRY — Office supplies ka bill Rs 25,000:
Dr  Office Supplies Expense    25,000   (PK 40 jaisi line, G/L)
    Cr  Vendor: Ahmed Traders          25,000   (PK 31)
Document Type: KR (Vendor Invoice)  ← KR = Kreditor Rechnung (German!)
Vendor ke khate mein ye amount OPEN ITEM ban gaya —
payment ki muntazir.

Step 4 — Payment: Manual vs Automatic

Manual Payment

F-53 — Outgoing payment post karna (ek vendor, haath se).
F-58 — Payment ke saath check print.
Entry: Vendor Dr / Bank Cr — aur saath invoice clear bhi ho jati hai.

Automatic Payment Program (APP)

F110 — SAP khud saare due vendors ki list bana kar, discount dekh kar, ek saath sab ko payment kar deta hai. Bari companies mein hafta-waar F110 "run" hota hai.

F110 — Automatic Payment Program step by step

  1. Parameters — Run Date + Identification likhein; company code, payment methods, aur next posting date dein; kin vendors ko shamil karna hai wo range dein.
  2. Proposal — SAP ek proposal list banata hai: kis vendor ko kitna, kis date ko, discount ke saath ya baghair. Ye abhi sirf tajweez (proposal) hai — paisay nahi gaye.
  3. Edit Proposal — Accountant check karta hai; kisi vendor ko block kar sakta hai (misal: dispute chal raha hai), kisi ki amount/date badal sakta hai.
  4. Payment Run — Ab asal postings banti hain: har vendor debit, bank clearing credit; invoices clear ho jati hain.
  5. Print / Medium — Checks print hote hain ya bank file (DME — Data Medium Exchange) banti hai jo bank ko bhejte hain.
Key Terms — F110 setup F110 chalane se pehle configuration zaroori hai (FBZP): Payment Methods (C = Check, T = Transfer...), House Bank (company ka apna bank account SAP mein), Bank Determination (kis bank se payment jayegi), aur paying company code. Vendor master mein bhi payment method aur bank details hone chahiyein — warna vendor proposal mein exception list (error list) mein chala jata hai.
Exam Trap #7 (1) Proposal run posting NAHI karta — sirf list banata hai; posting payment run se hoti hai. (2) F110 hamesha due date aur discount date dekh kar decide karta hai ke aaj kis ko dena hai. (3) Jo item payment block mein ho, wo F110 mein select nahi hota. (4) F110 se bani payment document invoices ko khud clear kar deti hai — alag se F-44 nahi chalana parta.

Step 5 — Clearing aur AP reports

  • F-44 — Vendor ke open items ko manually clear karna (misal: credit memo ko invoice se match karna).
  • FBL1N — Vendor Line Items: kisi vendor ki saari open/cleared entries.
  • F.13 — Automatic clearing (GR/IR jaise accounts ke liye).
  • FK10N — Vendor Balances (FS10N ka AP version).
  • Vendor ageing — kitna purana udhaar baaqi hai (current / 30 / 60 / 90 din) — reports S_ALR_87012084 (open items ageing) waghera se.
  • Down Payment (advance): vendor ko pehle paisay dena — F-48 se post hota hai, special G/L indicator "A" ke saath, taake advance alag nazar aye aur baad mein invoice se adjust ho jaye.
Analogy — F110 ek "salary day" hai Jaise company har mahine ek din saare mulazimon ki salary ek saath transfer karti hai — list banti hai, manager approve karta hai, phir ek click se sab ke accounts mein paisay. F110 vendors ke liye bilkul waisa hi "payment day" hai: proposal = salary list, edit = manager ki checking, payment run = transfer wala din.
Memory Trick — AP T-codes AP mein K har jagah hai (German Kreditor = vendor): FB60 ki document type KR, account type K, vendor balances FK10N, line items FBL1N. Aur F110 ko yaad karein "1-1-0 = ek saath sab ko payment, emergency speed se!"

Exam Traps, Glossary, Summary & Cheat Sheet

C_TS4FI revision · Poora Lesson 10 ek jagah

Saare Exam Traps — ek list

  1. Blank employee tolerance group (OBA4) lazmi define karein — warna "no tolerance group" error.
  2. Payment difference mein amount AUR % dono limits check hoti hain; employee aur vendor tolerance mein se sakht tareen apply hoti hai.
  3. Residual: invoice clear + naya open item (nayi due date). Partial: invoice open rehti hai (purani due date).
  4. Parked document: number milta hai, balance update nahi, delete ho sakta hai. Posted: delete nahi, sirf reverse (FB08). Held: number bhi nahi milta.
  5. FS10N = balances, FBL3N = line items, FAGLL03 = ledger-wise line items.
  6. F-02 posting keys: 40 Dr G/L, 50 Cr G/L, 31 Cr Vendor, 21 Dr Vendor, 01 Dr Customer, 11 Cr Customer.
  7. Document date ≠ Posting date — period posting date se decide hota hai.
  8. Recon account mein direct posting nahi hoti — hamesha vendor/customer ke through.
  9. S/4HANA mein vendor = Business Partner (role FLVN00).
  10. F110: proposal ≠ posting; payment run post karta hai aur clear bhi.
  11. Payment terms mein baseline date se din ginte hain; discount pehle, net due date baad mein.
  12. Down payment special G/L indicator A se post hota hai (F-48) — aam open item nahi banta.

Summary — Lesson 10 panch (5) lines mein

  1. Tolerance = chhote farq ki bardasht limit — teen groups: Employee (OBA4), G/L (OBA0), Vendor/Customer (OBA3). Farq handle karne ke tareeqay: write off, residual item, partial payment.
  2. Park = entry save lekin final nahi — check/approval ke liye; Post = final. Hold us se bhi kacha hai.
  3. G/L Reporting: FS10N balances, FBL3N/FAGLL03 line items, drilldown se har number ki jar tak.
  4. F-02 = classic general posting; posting keys 40/50 (G/L), 31/21 (vendor), 01/11 (customer); debit = credit lazmi.
  5. AP = vendor master (BP, recon account, payment terms OBB8) → invoice (FB60/MIRO) → payment (F-53 manual / F110 automatic) → clearing (F-44). Recon account kabhi direct post nahi hota.
